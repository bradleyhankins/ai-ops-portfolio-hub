# Privacy and AI Use

Practical AI Ops Toolkit is a public portfolio hub for AI-enhanced workflow applications. It is designed for fictional, sample, or non-sensitive business workflow scenarios.

## User Data Guidance

Do not enter sensitive, confidential, regulated, or private information into the public demos.

Avoid entering or uploading:

- Customer personally identifiable information
- Employee or applicant confidential records
- Compensation or payroll data
- Medical, legal, or regulated data
- Internal company secrets
- Proprietary pricing, contracts, or confidential business records
- Passwords, API keys, or credentials

## AI Processing

When an OpenAI token is configured, selected user inputs may be sent to the AI provider to improve summaries, recommendations, reports, and communication outputs.

The toolkit is built so that:

- Rules-based workflow logic is the source of truth
- AI only improves wording, clarity, tone, and structure
- AI failure falls back silently to rules-based output
- The apps do not intentionally store user-submitted data

## Portfolio Routing

Portfolio Hub uses rules-based routing to recommend the best app for the selected business need. AI may improve the wording of the recommendation, but it should not invent features, links, project status, or technical capabilities.

## Public Demo Note

All built-in sample names, companies, and scenarios are fictional and created for portfolio demonstration purposes.
